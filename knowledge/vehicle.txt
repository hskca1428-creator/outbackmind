[Title] Vehicle breakdown safety
Stay with your vehicle. It provides shelter and is easier for rescuers to locate than a person on foot.

[Title] Spare tyre preparation
Always carry at least one spare tyre, ideally two in remote areas. Check tyre pressure regularly.

[Title] Fuel planning
Carry extra fuel when travelling remote routes. Fuel stations may be hundreds of kilometres apart.

[Title] Engine overheating
If your engine overheats, stop immediately, turn off the engine, and allow it to cool before opening the radiator.

[Title] Battery failure
Carry jumper cables or a portable jump starter. Avoid draining the battery unnecessarily.

[Title] Night driving risks
Wildlife is more active at night. Reduce speed and stay alert for kangaroos and other animals.

[Title] Recovery gear
Carry basic recovery gear such as a shovel, traction boards, and tow straps when travelling off-road.

[Title] Navigation backup
Do not rely solely on GPS or mobile phones. Carry physical maps or offline navigation tools.
