[Title] Trip planning basics
Plan your route in advance and inform someone of your travel plans.

[Title] Weather checks
Check weather forecasts before and during travel.

[Title] Road conditions
Monitor road closures and conditions, especially after rain.

[Title] Communication planning
Carry satellite communication devices for remote travel.

[Title] Travel timing
Avoid travelling during extreme heat or dangerous conditions.

[Title] Emergency contacts
Keep a list of emergency contacts and local services.

[Title] Packing essentials
Include water, food, first aid, tools, and spare parts.

[Title] Backup plans
Always have an alternative route or plan.
