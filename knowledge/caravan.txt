[Title] Caravan weight limits
Do not exceed your caravan’s weight limits. Overloading can lead to dangerous handling and tyre failure.

[Title] Hitch safety
Ensure the hitch is properly secured and checked before every trip.

[Title] Water tank management
Monitor water levels and refill regularly, especially in remote areas.

[Title] Battery systems
Check battery charge levels and solar systems regularly.

[Title] Brake systems
Ensure caravan brakes are working properly before long trips.

[Title] Tyre maintenance
Inspect caravan tyres for wear, cracks, and pressure regularly.

[Title] Setup safety
Level your caravan and secure it properly at campsites.

[Title] Waste disposal
Dispose of waste responsibly at designated facilities.
