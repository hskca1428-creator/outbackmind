[Title] Lost in the bush
Stay in one place if possible. Use signals like fire, mirrors, or bright clothing to attract attention.

[Title] Shelter building
Create shade using tarps, branches, or your vehicle to protect from sun exposure.

[Title] Water sourcing
Water sources may be unreliable. Always carry sufficient water rather than relying on finding it.

[Title] Fire starting
Carry reliable fire-starting tools such as lighters, waterproof matches, or fire starters.

[Title] Food planning
Bring non-perishable food like canned goods, dried meals, and high-energy snacks.

[Title] Wildlife awareness
Keep food sealed and avoid feeding wildlife. Maintain a safe distance from all animals.

[Title] Emergency signalling
Use mirrors, whistles, and bright materials to signal for help.

[Title] Staying calm
Panic leads to poor decisions. Stay calm, think clearly, and conserve energy.
