How to use it

Use Normal Mode for planning, gear, routes, and caravan travel. Use Emergency Mode when the situation needs immediate, action-first instructions.

  • Use short, direct questions for urgent situations.
  • Ask about water, fuel, heat, wildlife, or breakdowns.
  • Refresh history to review recent guidance.
  • Treat emergency advice as a guide, not a replacement for official emergency services.
